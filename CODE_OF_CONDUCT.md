# Community conduct

Contributors and users should be treated respectfully regardless of identity, background or level of experience. Critique code and arguments, not people. Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, publication of private information and repeated disruptive behavior are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful material and restrict participation when necessary. Responses should be proportionate to the behavior and the need to protect participants. Maintainers are subject to the same standards.

Report concerns privately to a repository maintainer through a contact channel they publish. Do not put private information into an issue. This source template does not invent a monitored contact address; maintainers should add their actual reporting channel before opening the community broadly.
